Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about JobStrider before downloading.

Is JobStrider genuinely 100% free?
Yes! JobStrider runs directly on your computer, meaning we have zero cloud server bills. You use your own free Google Gemini API key (which provides 1,500 free evaluations every day with zero credit card required).
Why a Desktop App (.exe) instead of a regular website?
Websites require uploading your sensitive resume, phone number, and address to someone else's server. A desktop app guarantees 100% privacy: your files and job application history never leave your machine.
Why does Windows show "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen)?
This is completely standard for newly released open-source software. Microsoft Defender SmartScreen flags applications that don't purchase expensive $400/year corporate code-signing certificates. To install: simply click "More info" and then click "Run anyway". All JobStrider source code is 100% public, open-source, and auditable on GitHub.
Can my LinkedIn or Naukri account get banned?
Zero chance. JobStrider never asks for your LinkedIn or Naukri passwords or session cookies. It queries public job board APIs (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) directly from your normal internet connection.
How do I get a Google Gemini API key?
Go to Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com), sign in with any Google account, and click "Get API Key". It takes less than 60 seconds and is completely free forever.
